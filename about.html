About Poniente Springs

A woodland spring sanctuary, preserved by nature.

Our Roots

Nurtured by natural cold springs

Poniente Springs began as a hidden natural haven in Brgy. Pangil, Amadeo—a town celebrated for its cool highland climate, coffee farms, and pure groundwater rivers. fed by natural underground currents, the waters flow continually through clean stone basins without heavy commercial chemicals.

What started as a quiet local retreat slowly grew into a beloved resort destination, thoughtfully developed so as to preserve the surrounding forest, mature trees, and natural topography.

Today

A home for campers & nature lovers

Over the years, we expanded our grounds to accommodate car camping, tent pitch spots, open-air picnic cottages, and family-friendly spring pools. We maintain a simple philosophy: keep the resort rustic, uncluttered, and connected to the outdoors.

Whether you are dipping into the refreshing pools on a warm afternoon or setting up camp under the starry Cavite night sky, Poniente Springs offers an authentic escape back to simplicity.

Resort Guidelines

What keeps the sanctuary special

100% Flowing Natural Spring Water

Our pools are refreshed by live mountain spring water, giving visitors a crisp, natural dip that reenergizes the body and mind.

Leave No Trace, Protect the Greens

We ask all guests and campers to respect the surrounding flora, keep noise levels peaceful, and dispose of trash responsibly.

Family-First Outdoor Gathering

From day-tour picnic sheds to overnight car camping spots, our space is built to bring families and friends together comfortably.

Getting Here

Amadeo, Cavite, Philippines

Poniente Springs Resort is located in Brgy. Pangil, Amadeo, Cavite—just a short, scenic drive from Metro Manila or Tagaytay City via Crisanto M. De Los Reyes Avenue. Accessible by private vehicle, motorbikes, and car-camping rigs.

From Tagaytay ~20 mins via Amadeo Road
From Manila ~1.5 to 2 hrs via CALAX / SLEX
