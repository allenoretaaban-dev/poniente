01 — The Environment

Pure mineral spring water straight from nature's source.

Tucked away in the cooler highlands of Amadeo, Cavite, Poniente Springs is famous for its invigorating, unadulterated spring pools. Surrounded by mature trees and earthy charm, it provides a refreshing sanctuary away from artificial chlorine and crowded commercial water parks.

Amadeo Heritage

Rooted in local nature

Located in the heart of Cavite’s agricultural and spring country, Poniente Springs was built to celebrate the pure spring water running beneath the town. Long before commercial resorts dominated the region, these natural basins served as refreshing rest stops for travelers and locals alike.

Today, the resort retains its rustic, organic charm—preserving the towering trees and natural terrain so visitors can truly disconnect and unwind.

The Experience

Simple, clean, and refreshing

Whether you are planning a quick weekend dip with friends or setting up your car camp for a quiet overnight retreat, Poniente Springs offers a back-to-nature atmosphere that welcomes everyone.

Leave behind the noise of the metro and spend your day listening to rustling leaves and flowing spring water.
